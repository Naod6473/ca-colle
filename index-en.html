Ctrl + C
  1. Open the full ad. On Indeed, click the job title to have it alone on the page. On LinkedIn, first click "See more" under the description.
  2. Select the text from the job title down to the profile wanted.
  3. Copy (Ctrl + C, or Cmd + C on a Mac) then paste here (Ctrl + V).
  4. On a phone: long-press the text, "Select all", "Copy". Or take a screenshot and attach it.

No problem if you copy too much: the app removes the website's buttons, menus and notices by itself.

My offers

    Your CV stays the same: compare it with several offers.

    How it works
    1. You paste the job offer, or attach it as a PDF or screenshot.
    2. You add your CV: PDF, Word or photo.
    3. You see what fits, what is missing and what needs proof.
    4. You improve your CV with sentences to fill in.
    5. You write your cover letter from your strengths.

    Your CV never leaves your device: everything is read in your browser.
